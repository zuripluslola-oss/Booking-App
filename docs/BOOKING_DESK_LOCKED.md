# Booking Desk — Locked Product Direction

Status: **Approved and locked**

The Booking Desk calendar is the heart of the business-side product. The uploaded interactive prototype `booking-desk-grid.html` is the visual and behavioral reference for implementation.

## Locked visual direction

- Professional front-desk workspace inspired by the clarity and density of leading salon systems, without copying any product.
- Staff displayed in vertical scheduling columns.
- Time displayed down the left side.
- Color-coded appointment blocks.
- Live current-time indicator.
- Dark plum, mauve, clay, and cream visual system from the approved prototype.
- Tablet-friendly front-desk layout and responsive mobile behavior.
- Booking Desk, Calendar, and Clients are active product areas.
- Payments, Reports, Inventory, Marketing, POS, and Payroll remain later scaling phases unless separately approved.

## Locked booking behavior

- Day navigation.
- Profession-specific calendar examples.
- Staff utilization shown by column.
- Color and status differences for confirmed, new, walk-in, completed, checked-in, current, and no-show appointments.
- Visible open gaps.
- Tap an open gap to begin a booking.
- Drag an appointment into a valid gap to reschedule.
- Reject moves when the service duration does not fit.
- Appointment action sheet for check-in, reminders, rescheduling, and no-show status.
- New appointment, block time, and find opening controls.
- At-a-glance cards for next appointment, booked percentage, first opening, and walk-ins.
- Side information for utilization, no-show watch, and ratings.

## Product rule

Every approved prototype, design decision, feature, or code change for this project must be saved and committed to the `zuripluslola-oss/Booking-App` GitHub repository. Important product work must not exist only in chat.

## Development priority

Build the excellent booking and scheduling platform first. The broader Meevo-class operating system—including full POS, inventory, payroll, vendor management, advanced marketing, and enterprise reporting—comes later during scaling.
