# Esthetician Profession Pack — Locked

## Purpose

The esthetician profession pack controls onboarding, services, booking rules, intake, consultation, treatment records, website sections, and approved template defaults for skincare and aesthetic businesses.

The supplied references establish two useful service-menu directions:

- a large visual menu with categories, prices, descriptions, add-ons, and Book now actions
- a cleaner mobile accordion with treatment categories and nested services

The platform will combine the strengths of both while correcting the mobile clipping and narrow-column problems visible in the references.

## Recommended website templates

Default template: **Clinical Aesthetic**

Additional recommended choices:

- Soft Spa
- Service Menu
- Boutique Minimal
- Editorial Luxe

All ten platform templates remain selectable, but these six receive esthetician-specific previews and defaults.

## Service menu behavior

Services are grouped into collapsible categories on mobile and may display as cards, an editorial menu, or accordions depending on template.

Each service may include:

- name
- category
- short description
- full description
- duration
- starting price or fixed price
- deposit
- consultation required
- approval required
- patch test required
- contraindications
- preparation instructions
- aftercare instructions
- add-ons
- recommended frequency
- package eligibility
- membership eligibility
- staff eligibility
- room or equipment requirement
- image or video
- booking availability

Long names and descriptions wrap naturally. Prices remain aligned and never split awkwardly into narrow columns.

## Med-spa boundary

The esthetician pack does not automatically authorize medical services.

Injectables, prescription products, medical weight loss, ablative or medical lasers, PDO threads, and other medical procedures belong to a separate **Medical Aesthetics / Med Spa** eligibility layer. They may appear only when the business records the required provider type, licenses, location eligibility, supervision, consent forms, and applicable service permissions.

This preserves the Clinical Aesthetic website style without treating all estheticians as medical providers.

## Definition of done

The esthetician profession pack is complete for version 1 when a business can:

- select esthetician during onboarding
- receive a professional starting service catalog
- edit, remove, hide, and add services and categories
- configure consultations, forms, deposits, rooms, equipment, and eligibility
- choose among the ten website templates
- publish a mobile-safe service menu
- accept eligible bookings
- store treatment records and photos
- send preparation and aftercare information
- sell packages or memberships when enabled

This is the locked esthetician direction.
